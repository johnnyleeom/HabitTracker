Notification Troubleshooting

  • Confirm that notifications are enabled for StreakOn in your iPhone Settings.
  • Confirm that the habit has a reminder time and scheduled days.
  • Check whether Focus mode or notification summaries are delaying the notification.
  • Open StreakOn after reinstalling the app so notification permissions and reminders can be configured again.

Sign-In Issues

  • Confirm that your email address and password are correct.
  • Check that your device has an active internet connection.
  • If your account was permanently deleted, it cannot be restored. You may create a new account using the same email address.

Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your StreakOn account directly inside the app:

  1. Open StreakOn.
  2. Open the Settings tab.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion.

Deleting your account permanently removes your authentication account, habits, and progress history. This action cannot be undone.
