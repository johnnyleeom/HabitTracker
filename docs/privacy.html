HABITFLOW

Privacy Policy

Effective August 12, 2026

Overview

HabitFlow respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, why it is collected, how it is handled, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

  • Email address: Used to create and authenticate your account.
  • Habit information: Habit names, selected days, reminder times, and creation dates.
  • Progress information: Habit completion history and responses recorded within the app.
  • Account identifiers: A unique identifier used to associate your habits with your account.
  • Technical information: Our service providers may automatically process limited technical information, such as IP addresses, request logs, and error information, to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

HabitFlow does not collect payment information, precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

  • Create and authenticate your account
  • Store and display your habits
  • Schedule habit reminders on your device
  • Record and display your progress and streaks
  • Operate, secure, and maintain the app
  • Respond to support and privacy requests

HabitFlow does not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Notifications

Habit reminders are scheduled locally on your device based on the days and times you select. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

Service Providers

HabitFlow uses the following service providers:

  • Supabase: Provides account authentication and database storage.
  • Render: Hosts the backend server that processes authenticated requests.

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services and according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

HabitFlow uses reasonable safeguards designed to protect your information, including encrypted HTTPS connections, authenticated requests, database access controls, and Row Level Security policies.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention and Account Deletion

Your account information, habits, and progress history are retained while your account remains active.

You may permanently delete your account from the Settings screen in HabitFlow. Deleting your account removes your authentication account and associated habits and progress history. Account deletion cannot be undone.

Your Choices

  • Update or remove habits within the app
  • Disable notifications through your device settings
  • Sign out of your account
  • Permanently delete your account and associated data
  • Contact us with questions about your information

Children’s Privacy

HabitFlow is not directed to children under the age of 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when HabitFlow or its data practices change. The updated policy will be posted here with a revised effective date.

Contact Us
